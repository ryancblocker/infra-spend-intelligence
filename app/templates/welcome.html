Find the money hiding in your infrastructure contracts.

PACT runs eight agents over a portfolio of telecom, colocation, software and mobility contracts — reading the actual contract language, checking rates against market benchmarks, and recommending what to keep, cancel or renegotiate. It runs entirely on your machine against a local model. No contract text leaves this computer.
