CONTRACT ID: C-0037
VENDOR: MobileOne
CATEGORY: Mobile Services

This Enterprise Mobility Services Agreement ('Agreement') governs wireless voice and data service for Customer's workforce, covering 37 active lines.

1. TERM AND RENEWAL. This Agreement commences on 2025-11-15 and continues through 2027-11-15 (the 'Term'), unless earlier terminated as provided herein. This Agreement shall terminate at the end of the Term unless renewed by mutual written agreement of the parties at least 45 days prior to expiration.

2. FEES. Customer shall pay Vendor recurring fees of $1,900.06 per month ($22,800.72 annualized), subject to an Annual Escalator of 3% applied on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

3. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement for uncured material breach upon thirty (30) days' written notice. If Customer terminates for convenience prior to the end of the Term, Customer shall pay an early termination fee equal to 20% of the remaining committed charges under the Term.

4. Service Level Credits: In the event measured availability falls below 99.95% in a calendar month, Customer shall receive a credit of 2% of that month's fees per 0.1% shortfall, capped at 25% of monthly fees.

5. Limitation of Liability: Except for breaches of confidentiality or indemnification obligations, each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

6. Data Processing: The parties' Data Processing Addendum, incorporated by reference, governs the processing of Customer Data, including applicable cross-border transfer mechanisms and breach notification timelines.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Customer's APAC headquarters is located, without regard to conflict of law principles.
