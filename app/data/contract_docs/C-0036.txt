CONTRACT ID: C-0036
VENDOR: Synapse Copilot
CATEGORY: Software License

This Software Subscription Agreement ('Agreement') governs Customer's subscription to Vendor's software products on a 540 committed seats (consumption true-up quarterly) basis.

1. TERM AND RENEWAL. This Agreement commences on 2023-09-04 and continues through 2026-09-03 (the 'Term'), unless earlier terminated as provided herein. This Agreement shall terminate at the end of the Term unless renewed by mutual written agreement of the parties at least 45 days prior to expiration.

2. FEES. Customer shall pay Vendor recurring fees of $57,453.18 per month ($689,438.16 annualized), subject to an Annual Escalator of 6% applied on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

3. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement for uncured material breach upon thirty (30) days' written notice. If Customer terminates for convenience prior to the end of the Term, Customer shall pay an early termination fee equal to 18% of the remaining committed charges under the Term.

4. Service Level Credits: In the event measured availability falls below 99.95% in a calendar month, Customer shall receive a credit of 2% of that month's fees per 0.1% shortfall, capped at 25% of monthly fees.

5. Limitation of Liability: Except for breaches of confidentiality or indemnification obligations, each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

6. Most Favored Pricing: Vendor represents that the pricing herein is no less favorable than pricing offered to similarly situated customers of comparable volume. Should Vendor extend more favorable pricing to a similarly situated customer during the Term, Vendor shall extend equivalent terms to Customer upon written request.

7. Data Processing: The parties' Data Processing Addendum, incorporated by reference, governs the processing of Customer Data, including applicable cross-border transfer mechanisms and breach notification timelines.

8. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Customer's US-Central headquarters is located, without regard to conflict of law principles.
