# PACT Demo Script

## Demo Storyline
1. Open Executive Dashboard and highlight total spend and projected savings.
2. Navigate to Savings Opportunities and show underutilized assets.
3. Navigate to Renewal Risks and explain notice windows.
4. Navigate to Optimization Scenarios and compare three strategic options.
5. Navigate to Agent Results and show full transparency of each stage.

## Key Talking Points
- PACT creates a single source of truth for infra contract economics.
- Agents continuously surface waste, risk, and negotiation opportunities.
- Scenario modeling ties operational decisions to financial outcomes.
